# Unslop catalog

Read only by the one-shot rewrite (`/unslop` with a text or a file). The always-on mode never reads it.
Entry form: tell, example, fix. A tell is a signal, not proof: one hit alone is weak, a cluster is strong.

## Rules for every edit

- Cut a tic. Add nothing. No personality, joke, reaction, fragment, rhetorical question, first-person
  experience, forced take or invented detail. No new sentence that was not in the original.
- Never delete a negation, a hedge that carries real uncertainty, a number, a unit or a caveat. One
  exception: the negation inside a "not X but Y" frame you remove, when Y alone states the claim.
- Technical carve-out: inside code, commands, logs, quoted errors and terms of art, nothing is a tic
  (`robust` in "robust statistics", `gate` in "quality gate", `leverage` in finance, `landscape` in page layout).
- Not tics alone: correct grammar, formal words, one "however", one dash, one short emphatic sentence, a
  rhetorical question a human would ask, a triad of three real things.
- Do not over-clean. Text with no voice at all is as machine-made as text with tells.
- Deletion test: cut the frame. If no information is lost, it was never content.
- No absolutes: no ban on all adverbs, passive voice, triads, "never" or "always".
  They are often the fact.

## 1. Contrast frames

- "Not X but Y". Ex: "It's not a bug, it's a design choice." Fix: state Y.
- Split form. Ex: "This doesn't mean X. It means Y." Fix: state Y.
- Staged correction. Ex: "The real story isn't speed. It's trust." Fix: state the claim.
- "Not only X but also Y", "not merely". Fix: one clause with both facts, or drop the weak half.
- "No X, no Y" chains. Ex: "No fluff. No filler. No jargon." Fix: say the one thing that is true.
- Clipped tail. Ex: "..., no guessing." Fix: cut it.
- Keep a contrast when it corrects a belief the reader holds, or both halves carry facts. Ex: "It is not a
  timeout, it is a refused connection" stays.
- RU: «не просто X, а Y», «не только X, но и Y», «это не X, это Y». Same test, same fix.

## 2. Formatting

- Bold-label bullets. Ex: "- **Speed:** it is faster." Fix: prose, unless the labels carry information.
- Decorative bold on key terms. Fix: at most one bold phrase per section, or none.
- Emoji in headings and bullets. Fix: remove.
- Scaffold headers on a short answer ("Overview", "Key points", "Summary", "Conclusion"). Fix: remove.
- A heading per two sentences, or a heading plus a one-line restatement. Fix: merge into paragraphs.
- Bullets for content that is not a list (8 bullets in 200 words). Fix: prose. Lists stay for steps,
  parameters and comparisons.
- A rule between every section, a title heading that repeats the title. Fix: remove.
- RU: the echo colon, "Слово: развёрнутое повторение слова". Fix: write the sentence.

## 3. Chat residue

- Closing offers. Ex: "Let me know if you need anything else." "Want me to...?" "Should I continue?"
  Fix: cut. Ask only when the next step needs a decision.
- Agreement openers. Ex: "You're absolutely right!" "Great catch." Fix: cut. If the user was right, act.
- Restating the question or the user's work. Ex: "You're asking about caching." Fix: answer.
- Narrated candor. Ex: "To be honest", "Honestly?", "I want to be upfront". Fix: cut the frame, keep the
  statement. A real admission stays: "I have not tested this on Windows."
- Staged run-ups. Ex: "Here's the thing", "Let me be clear", "Make no mistake", "Let's dive in". Fix: cut.
- Reasoning leaks. Ex: "Let me think step by step", "Breaking this down". Fix: give the result.
- Arguing with no one. Ex: "Don't get me wrong", "You might think X". Fix: cut.
- RU: «Если хотите, могу...», «Хотите, чтобы я...?», «Вы абсолютно правы», «Отличный вопрос», «Честно
  говоря», «Надеюсь, это поможет». Fix: cut.

## 4. Structure and inflation

- Aphorism closer. Ex: "That is the real win." "Read that again." Fix: cut, or end on the last fact.
- Staccato fragment runs. Ex: "No prior. No habit. Just data." Fix: one full sentence.
- Deep-sounding formulas. Ex: "at its core", "the real question is", "what really matters". Fix: cut.
- Performed insight. Ex: "Sit with that.", "The punchline:", "Turns out...". Fix: state the fact.
- Significance inflation. Ex: "a pivotal moment", "stands as a testament", "plays a crucial role". Fix:
  state what happened or what it does, with a number if one exists.
- Vague attribution. Ex: "experts say", "studies show", "some critics". Fix: name the source or drop the
  claim. Do not invent a source.
- False agency. Ex: "the data tells us", "the decision emerges". Fix: name the actor ("we read the data as").
- Copula avoidance. Ex: "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features". Fix: "is", "has".
- Trailing -ing rider. Ex: "..., highlighting the need for care." Fix: cut, or make it its own sentence if
  it is a real claim.
- False range. Ex: "from onboarding to scale". Fix: list the actual items.
- Forced triad. Ex: "faster, simpler, and more reliable" with one real fact behind it. Fix: keep what is real.
- Repeated sentence openers (three or more with the same first word). Fix: vary or merge.
- Weasel quantifiers. Ex: "a wide range of", "numerous", "a plethora of". Fix: a number, or "many".
- Empty endorsement. Ex: "worth exploring". Fix: say why, or cut.
- RU: «играет важную роль», «подчёркивает важность», «данные говорят», «решение рождается». Same fixes.

## 5. Dashes

- English: at most one em dash per paragraph. Never as clause glue where a comma or period works. Never a
  spaced `--` as a dash. Keep dashes in code, paths, URLs, quotes and ranges.
- Russian and Ukrainian: the grammatical dash stays ("Москва - столица", dialogue, apposition, ranges). Only a
  run of clause-glue dashes in one paragraph is the tic. Fix: commas and periods.

## 6. English words (capped; the technical carve-out applies)

pivotal, crucial, testament, underscore, showcase, foster, elevate, unlock, navigate (figurative), holistic,
comprehensive, cutting-edge, meticulous, intricate, nuanced, game-changer, journey (figurative), tapestry,
delve, realm, landscape (figurative), seamless, robust (figurative), leverage (verb).
Hollow intensifiers: genuinely, truly, literally, fundamentally, crucially, importantly, interestingly,
notably. `actually` stays when it marks a real correction ("It was actually a miss").
Fix: a plain word, or cut. Word habits change with each model. Structure persists, so check structure first.

## 7. Russian hard bans (own list; fix is the plain statement, or cut)

- «играет ключевую/важную роль», «раскрыть потенциал», «комплексный подход/решение»
- «в современном мире», «в условиях...», «на сегодняшний день»
- «можно с уверенностью сказать», «подводя итог», «в заключение»
- «вывести на новый уровень», «открывает новые горизонты/возможности»
- «погрузимся в...», «и вот здесь начинается самое интересное»
- «важно помнить/отметить/понимать, что», «стоит отметить», «в связи с этим»
- Канцелярит nouns: осуществление, реализация, внедрение, оптимизация, функционирование, обеспечение.
  Connectives: «в контексте», «на основе», «посредством», «в соответствии с». Fix: a verb.
- «является»: a density rule, not a ban. More than about one per 500 words: use «это» or a verb.
- Do not add particles or "liveliness" words to sound alive. That reads foreign.
