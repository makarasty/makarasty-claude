00 · How to run it

The rules, and the five lines for when you are lost

Who is on the line and what they can do. What they cannot do, so no question asks them to.

Rule 1

We ask, we do not propose

We do this, we get this, please explain why and how it should work.

Rule 2

Leave them the exit

End a hard question with “Maybe we send it wrong, or read the wrong field. Please tell me.”

Rule 3

One line, then a pause

Each line in a green block is one sentence. Read it, stop, breathe.

Rule 4

Ask, then be quiet

After the question line, the next line is theirs.

Rule 5

Every answer in writing

“Can you send that to me in writing?”

Rule 6

Argue only with their own numbers

A request number, a ticket, a field name from their document.

Rule 7

Money stays off

They raise it: listen, write it down, name no figure of ours.

When you are lost: five lines that always work

Say again

“Sorry, can you say that again, a bit slower?”

Confirm

“Let me check I understood. You said the code is in the second answer. Is that right?”

Correction

“Correction. The right number is forty three, not forty two.”

Standby

“One moment, I am checking.”

Unable

“I cannot answer that now. I will write to you today.”
